# OrderPulse — Operational Investigation Plan

Historical Olist data. Recommendations are hypotheses, not proven root causes or measured improvements.

Overall late-delivery baseline: 6.77%.

## RJ
- Eligible orders: 12,350
- Late orders: 1,495
- Late rate: 12.11%
- Above-baseline count: 658.52
- Investigation: Investigate seller origins, fulfillment handoffs and monthly trends.
- Proposed pilot: Test earlier delay alerts for a defined shipment segment.
- Success measure: Compare late rate with a similar untreated group; track review coverage and intervention cost.

## Important limitations
- The baseline is descriptive and not adjusted for order mix.
- Above-baseline counts are not guaranteed preventable delays.
- Seller, carrier and route responsibility is not established.
- Any improvement must be measured in a real pilot.
